MudMix: HDD Mud Calculator — Support & Privacy

Mud mixes by rig and ground.

Support

MudMix is a mud-mix calculator for horizontal directional drilling: pick your rig (Ditch Witch or Vermeer), your ground type, and your tank size, and it gives you the mix — in pounds, bags, and quarts.

Questions or feedback about MudMix? Email us at generalobvious87@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

Privacy Policy

MudMix does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app works fully offline.
  • There are no user accounts.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking.
  • Nothing you enter in the app ever leaves your device.

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page. Questions about privacy: generalobvious87@gmail.com.
